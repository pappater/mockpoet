# On Unhurried Attention

> "What we do not hurry past becomes a landmark; what we label too quickly becomes invisible."

Every afternoon has an unguarded five minutes between tasks—just long enough to notice how slant light moves across a wall, or how a familiar sentence reveals a second hinge when read slowly. Patience is rarely passive; it is the discipline of letting meaning arrive at its natural speed.
