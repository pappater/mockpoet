# Series Bible: The Clockmaker's Alibi

- **Setting**: Ravenscar Junction, a fog-bound railway town in late autumn.
- **Characters**:
  - **ARTHUR VANCE** (58): Master clockmaker whose workshop overlooks Platform 1. Methodical, guarded.
  - **CLARA HENDERSON** (32): Railway telegraph clerk and amateur stenographer; sharp observer.
  - **INSPECTOR HARGREAVES** (47): County investigator sent to trace the disappearance of financier Julian Mercer on the 9:14 express.
  - **TOMAS** (21): Arthur's quiet apprentice, carrying a secret train ticket.
- **Central Object**: A gold pocket watch stopped at 9:11 PM—three minutes before the train departed.
