# Scene Summaries

## Act 1, Scene 1: The Stopped Pendulum

Clara warns clockmaker Arthur Vance that Inspector Hargreaves has arrived to investigate financier Julian Mercer's disappearance. She reveals an uncoded telegraph message sent at 9:11 PM—matching the exact minute Arthur's wall regulator clock stopped.

## Act 1, Scene 2: The Platform Ticket

Inspector Hargreaves produces a soot-smudged platform ticket stamped at 9:06 PM bearing traces of watchmaker's bone oil. Arthur points out that his missing apprentice Tomas had access to the oil shelf just as a floorboard creaks in the back room.
