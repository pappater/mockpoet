# Act 1, Scene 2: The Sealed Envelope

*Setting: The veranda dining table overlooking the rain-filled courtyard. Warm tungsten bulb light reflects off wet red-oxide flooring. RAGHAV sits at the head of the table in a damp button-down shirt, a thick Manila envelope resting unopened beside his brass spectacles.*

RAGHAV
I'm only going to say this once before we open it: whatever developer sent that valuation last month is offering twice what the plot is worth on paper, and zero for the nostalgia.

ARJUN
[Leaning against the wooden pillar, arms folded]
Let me guess—you already measured how many apartments fit between the well and the gate.

RAGHAV
Someone in this family has to look at a balance sheet without crying over chipped mosaic tiles, Arjun!

KAVYA
[Tapping her pen against her architectural pad]
Wait. Before you two start your usual duet, look at the seal on that envelope. That isn't the bank's wax. That's Appa's library stamp.

MEENAKSHI
[Sitting calmly in the cane armchair near the kitchen door, shelling peanuts into a brass bowl]
Open it, Raghav. Your father was many things—stubborn, late to weddings, terrible with umbrellas—but he never wrote a boring postscript.

[RAGHAV breaks the seal and unfolds a single sheet of cream stationery. As he reads silently, his shoulders drop an inch.]

ARJUN
Well? Did he leave the house to the cat?

RAGHAV
[Reading aloud slowly]
*"To Raghav, Kavya, and Arjun: The house on Monsoon Street may be sold, restored, or leased—on one condition only. Before a single wall is touched, the three thousand books in the upstairs library must be catalogued and given to people who will read them, one shelf per week, in person."*

KAVYA
One shelf per week? There are forty-eight shelves! That takes nearly a year.

MEENAKSHI
[Smiling quietly without looking up from her bowl]
Almost long enough to survive one monsoon and remember why you used to like living together.

[Outside, the courtyard rain steadies into a silver curtain as all three siblings stare at the staircase leading up to the dark library.]
