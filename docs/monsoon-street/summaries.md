# Scene Summaries

## Act 1, Scene 1: The Leaking Skylight

Architect Kavya returns home during a fierce November monsoon to find her brother Arjun catching roof drips in brass vessels. Aunt Meenakshi reveals their eldest brother Raghav is bringing a sealed envelope left by their late father.

## Act 1, Scene 2: The Sealed Envelope

Raghav arrives pushing to sell the ancestral home, but their father's letter stipulates that before any wall is touched, all three thousand books in the upstairs library must be catalogued and gifted in person, one shelf per week.
