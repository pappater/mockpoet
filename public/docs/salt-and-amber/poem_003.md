# Letters Never Mailed in Autumn

All summer we mistook abundance for permanence,
believing the chestnut tree would keep its heavy lamps
lit long past October.

Now the courtyard carries the crisp arithmetic of frost,
and in the desk drawer, half-finished sentences
grow wiser for having waited in the dark.

Do not grieve the page you folded twice and kept:
every unsent word becomes an inner room
where someone you once loved may still walk slowly
without the noise of footsteps.
