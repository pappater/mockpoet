# Series Bible: Glass Houses on Monsoon Street

- **Setting**: An ancestral courtyard house in Chennai during a relentless November monsoon.
- **Characters**:
  - **KAVYA** (36): Architect who returned from Bengaluru to assess whether the family house should be restored or sold.
  - **ARJUN** (33): Restless music archivist living in the upstairs study among three thousand books and vinyl records.
  - **MEENAKSHI** (64): Their sharp-witted aunt who knows every neighbor's history and refuses to let the brass tumblers leave the kitchen.
  - **RAGHAV** (39): The eldest brother, practical, overworked, arriving late with a lawyer's folder.
