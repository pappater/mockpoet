# Margins Are Where Readers Live

> "A book is finished by its author, yet it only begins to breathe when someone argues gently in its margins."

Unread volumes remain pristine monuments; read books collect bent corners, dates scribbled in pencil, and remembered train rides. Never fear leaving an honest trace beside a paragraph that startled you awake.
