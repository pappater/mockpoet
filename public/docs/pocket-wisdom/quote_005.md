# The Architecture of Small Promises

> "Grand declarations are easy to draft in moonlight; quiet reliability is written in ordinary Tuesdays."

Most friendships and crafts survive not on dramatic rescues, but on small, kept commitments—returning a borrowed book, answering a letter without delay, and showing up even when the weather offers no applause.
